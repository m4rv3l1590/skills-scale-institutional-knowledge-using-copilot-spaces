# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers ensure that products are intuitive, accessible, and provide excellent user experiences. They research user needs, create designs, prototypes, and collaborate with developers and product managers to deliver user-centered solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Design user interfaces that align with brand guidelines and accessibility standards
- Collaborate with developers to ensure design feasibility and quality implementation
- Maintain design systems and component libraries
- Validate designs with users and iterate based on feedback

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and improve satisfaction metrics
- Ensure consistency across product interfaces
- Advocate for user needs in product decisions

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with product and engineering teams
- User research findings and recommendations
- Design specifications and style guides
- Collaboration in sprint planning and refinement sessions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Product Managers** to understand user needs and business objectives
- Collaborates with **Developers** during implementation to ensure design fidelity
- Partners with **QA/Testing** to validate user experience quality
- Coordinates with **Documentation Specialists** on user-facing help content

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Release Engineers automate and optimize the software delivery pipeline. They manage infrastructure, CI/CD systems, deployments, and ensure reliable, repeatable releases with minimal downtime.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage deployment processes and release schedules
- Monitor system health, performance, and reliability
- Implement infrastructure as code and configuration management
- Coordinate deployments with development and operations teams
- Troubleshoot build, deployment, and infrastructure issues

### Goals
- Minimize deployment time and manual intervention
- Achieve high system reliability and uptime
- Enable fast, safe rollbacks when needed
- Reduce lead time from commit to production

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and deployment coordination
- Incident response and postmortems
- Infrastructure capacity planning with stakeholders
- CI/CD status updates and build notifications

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with **Developers** to optimize build and test processes
- Works with **Project Managers** on release planning and scheduling
- Partners with **Security Lead** to implement security scanning and compliance
- Coordinates with **Support/Customer Success** on deployment communications

---

## Documentation Specialist

### Role Summary
Documentation Specialists create, maintain, and improve technical and user documentation. They ensure that knowledge is accessible, accurate, and helpful for various audiences including users, developers, and support teams.

### Responsibilities
- Write and maintain technical documentation, user guides, and API references
- Collaborate with teams to capture institutional knowledge
- Establish documentation standards and style guides
- Review and edit content for clarity and accuracy
- Organize and maintain documentation repositories
- Gather feedback to continuously improve documentation

### Goals
- Make information discoverable and understandable
- Reduce support burden through self-service documentation
- Capture and scale institutional knowledge
- Maintain documentation consistency and quality

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews with subject matter experts
- Content planning sessions with product and engineering
- User feedback analysis and documentation improvements
- Contribution to knowledge base and internal wikis

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with **Product Managers** to document features and requirements
- Collaborates with **Developers** on technical documentation and API docs
- Partners with **UX/UI Designers** on user-facing help content
- Supports **Support/Customer Success** with customer-facing documentation
- Assists **Project Managers** with project documentation standards

---

## Support/Customer Success

### Role Summary
Support/Customer Success professionals ensure customers can effectively use the product and achieve their desired outcomes. They handle inquiries, provide guidance, escalate issues, and gather feedback to improve the product experience.

### Responsibilities
- Respond to customer inquiries and troubleshoot issues
- Document common problems and create knowledge base articles
- Escalate bugs and feature requests to engineering and product teams
- Track customer satisfaction and success metrics
- Provide onboarding and training for new customers
- Advocate for customer needs in product planning

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Reduce time to resolution for customer issues
- Identify and communicate product improvement opportunities
- Enable customer self-service through documentation

### Typical Communication
- Customer interactions via support channels (email, chat, phone)
- Weekly sync with product and engineering on common issues
- Customer feedback reports and feature requests
- Escalations and incident coordination

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with **Product Managers** to communicate customer needs and feedback
- Collaborates with **Developers** to troubleshoot and resolve issues
- Partners with **Documentation Specialists** to improve help content
- Coordinates with **DevOps/Release Engineers** during incidents and deployments
- Provides input to **UX/UI Designers** on usability issues

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure the product and infrastructure are secure, compliant, and resilient against threats. They establish security standards, review code and architecture, respond to vulnerabilities, and promote security awareness across the team.

### Responsibilities
- Define and enforce security standards and best practices
- Conduct security reviews of code, architecture, and infrastructure
- Manage vulnerability scanning and remediation processes
- Respond to security incidents and coordinate fixes
- Ensure compliance with relevant regulations and standards
- Provide security training and guidance to the team

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and attack surface
- Ensure compliance with security policies and regulations
- Build security into the development lifecycle
- Maintain rapid response capability for security issues

### Typical Communication
- Security review sessions with development teams
- Vulnerability reports and remediation tracking
- Security incident communications and postmortems
- Compliance status updates to stakeholders

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with **Developers** on secure coding practices and code reviews
- Works with **DevOps/Release Engineers** to implement security scanning and controls
- Partners with **Project Managers** to manage security-related risks
- Coordinates with **Product Managers** on security features and requirements
- Supports **Support/Customer Success** with security-related customer inquiries

## Onboarding Checklists for New Roles

### General Onboarding Steps (All Roles)
When bringing a new team member into any role, ensure these baseline activities are completed:

- [ ] Review the Project Management Overview document
- [ ] Understand the project lifecycle and current phase
- [ ] Meet with Project Manager for project context and timeline
- [ ] Review project charter and current roadmap
- [ ] Get access to relevant tools and systems
- [ ] Review communication cadence and meeting schedule
- [ ] Identify key stakeholders and introduce yourself
- [ ] Understand escalation paths and decision-making processes
- [ ] Review relevant documentation in the project repository
- [ ] Schedule regular check-ins with manager and key collaborators

### UX/UI Designer Specific Onboarding
- [ ] Access design tools (Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD, etc.)
- [ ] Review existing design system and component library
- [ ] Understand brand guidelines and accessibility standards
- [ ] Review past user research findings and personas
- [ ] Meet with Product Manager to understand product vision
- [ ] Connect with development team to understand technical constraints
- [ ] Review current design backlog and priorities
- [ ] Set up participation in design reviews and critiques

### DevOps/Release Engineer Specific Onboarding
- [ ] Get access to CI/CD systems and infrastructure
- [ ] Review deployment processes and release schedule
- [ ] Understand monitoring and alerting systems
- [ ] Review infrastructure as code repositories
- [ ] Meet with Security Lead to understand security requirements
- [ ] Learn about incident response procedures
- [ ] Review capacity planning and performance baselines
- [ ] Set up on-call rotation if applicable

### Documentation Specialist Specific Onboarding
- [ ] Access documentation platforms and repositories
- [ ] Review documentation standards and style guide
- [ ] Understand documentation workflow and review process
- [ ] Identify high-priority documentation gaps
- [ ] Meet with subject matter experts across teams
- [ ] Review analytics on documentation usage (if available)
- [ ] Understand the target audiences for different doc types
- [ ] Set up regular documentation review cycles

### Support/Customer Success Specific Onboarding
- [ ] Access support ticketing and CRM systems
- [ ] Review knowledge base and common customer issues
- [ ] Understand escalation procedures and SLAs
- [ ] Shadow experienced support team members
- [ ] Learn about the product from a customer perspective
- [ ] Review customer success metrics and goals
- [ ] Meet with Product Manager to understand roadmap
- [ ] Set up customer communication channels

### Security Lead Specific Onboarding
- [ ] Review security policies and compliance requirements
- [ ] Access security scanning and monitoring tools
- [ ] Understand current vulnerability management process
- [ ] Review recent security assessments and audits
- [ ] Meet with DevOps to understand infrastructure security
- [ ] Establish security review cadence with development team
- [ ] Review incident response plan and contact lists
- [ ] Understand data classification and handling procedures

## How Expanded Roles Improve Project Clarity and Accountability

Adding these specialized roles to the project team structure provides several benefits:

**Improved Clarity:**
- **Clear ownership** of specialized functions (security, documentation, user experience)
- **Defined interfaces** between roles reduce ambiguity about responsibilities
- **Explicit communication paths** ensure information flows to the right people
- **Standardized onboarding** helps new team members understand their role quickly

**Enhanced Accountability:**
- **Named owners** for critical non-functional requirements (security, usability, reliability)
- **Measurable outcomes** specific to each role (e.g., deployment frequency, documentation coverage, customer satisfaction)
- **Regular checkpoints** built into communication cadence for each role
- **Documented interactions** clarify how roles work together and where handoffs occur

**Better Project Outcomes:**
- **Reduced risks** through dedicated security and DevOps expertise
- **Higher quality** with specialized UX design and testing
- **Faster resolution** of customer issues with dedicated support
- **Knowledge retention** through systematic documentation

These roles ensure that critical aspects of project success—user experience, operational reliability, security, documentation, and customer satisfaction—have dedicated attention and accountability rather than being treated as secondary concerns.
